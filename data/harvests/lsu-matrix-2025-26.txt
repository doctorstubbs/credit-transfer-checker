Louisiana State University and A&M College, Louisiana Board of Regents Course Articulation Matrix, 2025-26 edition (public Power BI view, Home Courses page). Extracted Nov 11, 2026.
Method: the Home Institution filter (listed simply as LSU, the Baton Rouge flagship, distinct from LSUA, LSUE, LSUS, and LSU Health Sciences) was set and the table was read discipline by discipline (41 disciplines, 225 rows), scrolling inside each filtered table until rows stopped changing, then verified with a second full unfiltered sweep in overlapping steps, which corrected three first-pass errors (English rubric, the Mathematics list, the Latin list). The report's Home Course column shows codes only, so titles are the matrix Common Course Titles. Asterisks on some Common Course codes are the matrix's own footnote markers, reproduced in the rows file and stripped for matching.
