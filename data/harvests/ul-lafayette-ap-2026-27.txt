Source: 2026-2027 Undergraduate and Graduate Catalog (catalog.louisiana.edu, catoid=26), "Credit for Prior Learning", subsection C, Advanced Placement Program. Harvested Nov 8, 2026 via live browser and re-verified on reload.
Catalog warning on the section: "This section of the catalog is currently under review by the University. Changes are forthcoming and will be date stamped. The information below remains in effect until updates are made."
Policy points: grade of S awarded; credits count as hours earned and may satisfy degree requirements; students arrange to have scores sent to the Director of Admissions. No universal minimum stated in words; the lowest score listed in the table is 3 for every exam. No exam has an elective-only line.
