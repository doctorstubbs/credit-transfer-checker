Will this credit count?

A Louisiana college credit check: for community college transfers, dual enrollment and early college students, and anyone moving credits between Louisiana colleges. Built on the Louisiana Transfer Degree guarantee and the Board of Regents articulation rules. Version 1 covers the rules listed at the bottom of this page.

Where your credits come from

Add every college you earned credits at. Credits are evaluated college by college, the same way a registrar reads one transcript at a time. If you finished a transfer degree at a college, say so on that college's block.

Rules in this version: the Louisiana Transfer Degree block guarantee at public universities (general education complete, 60 hours, junior standing) with a grade of C or better in every course, attached to the college that granted the degree; a grade below C is flagged for the receiving university to decide; limited access program admission is separate; electives never replace a missing major prerequisite; course outcomes are direct equivalent, level elective, or general elective; private universities are not bound by the state guarantee; courses numbered below 100 earn no degree credit. Credits from several colleges are evaluated together and reported college by college. Courses can be checked two ways: finished courses are judged on the grade earned, and planned courses are answered with the condition stated plainly (the match is firm today; the counting is earned with a grade of C or better). This tool gives guidance from published rules, not an official ruling. The receiving university makes the final call on every credit.
