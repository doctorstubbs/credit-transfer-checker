About this check

A Louisiana college credit check: for community college transfers, dual enrollment and early college students, and anyone moving credits between Louisiana colleges. Built on the Board of Regents articulation rules and the colleges' own current catalogs. What this check assumes, and where its data comes from, is linked at the bottom of this page.

Where this data comes from, and what this check assumes

Rules in this version: course outcomes are direct equivalent, level elective, or general elective, from current college catalogs and the Louisiana articulation rules; private universities are not bound by the state guarantee; courses numbered below 100 earn no degree credit; limited access program admission is always a separate decision; electives never replace a missing major prerequisite. The Louisiana Transfer Degree block guarantee (general education complete, all 60 hours, junior standing) is not applied by this quick check: a degree is read from a transcript, not a dropdown, and that is what the full audit does. Credits from several colleges are evaluated together and reported college by college. This check assumes every course is passed with a grade of C or better, which is what transfer credit requires in the first place. If a course ends below a C, the receiving university decides that course on its own. Courses from colleges not yet in the course data are flagged as not covered yet rather than guessed. AP exams are checked under the Louisiana statewide AP policy: a public college awards credit for a score of 3 or higher once the official score report is sent; a score below 3, or a score report never sent, earns no credit; private universities set their own AP policy. This tool gives guidance from published rules, not an official ruling. The receiving university makes the final call on every credit.
