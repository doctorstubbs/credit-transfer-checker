Will this credit count?

A Louisiana dual enrollment credit check for families. Built on the Louisiana Transfer Degree guarantee and the Board of Regents articulation rules. Version 1 covers the rules listed at the bottom of this page.

For each course: its code (example ENGL 1010), the grade earned, and what a counselor would find in the articulation matrix: a direct match, no direct match in a subject the college teaches, or a subject the college does not offer.

Rules in this version: the Louisiana Transfer Degree block guarantee at public universities (general education complete, 60 hours, junior standing) with a grade of C or better in every course; a grade below C is flagged for the receiving university to decide; limited access program admission is separate; electives never replace a missing major prerequisite; course outcomes are direct equivalent, level elective, or general elective; private universities are not bound by the state guarantee; courses numbered below 100 earn no degree credit. This tool gives guidance from published rules, not an official ruling. The receiving university makes the final call on every credit.
